Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you have a website or web project, you have everything you need.

Can I put my app on Google Play?

Yes. Get an App Pass to publish one app with a single payment, or choose Pro or Business for all your apps. You publish from your own Google Play account.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. The App Pass is a one-time payment for one app, and every update to that app is free.

Who owns the app?

You do. Your app, your content, your name on the store.

Will it work without internet?

Yes. Apps made from your own files work fully offline, and website apps keep the pages you've already opened.
